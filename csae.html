Child Safety & CSAE Standards

These standards explain NzuKonect’s approach to preventing, reporting and responding to child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Effective date: 28 July 2026

Zero-tolerance policy: NzuKonect prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), grooming and any activity that sexualises, exploits or endangers a child.

1. Scope

These standards apply to all users, administrators, participating organisations, contributors and other persons accessing NzuKonect services.

2. Prohibited Content and Behaviour

The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Creating, uploading, possessing, storing, sharing, requesting or linking to CSAM.
  • Sexual content, imagery, conversation or activity involving a minor.
  • Grooming, coercion, enticement or solicitation of a minor.
  • Sexual harassment, threats, blackmail or exploitation involving a minor.
  • Attempts to arrange inappropriate or exploitative contact with a child.
  • Content that promotes, normalises, facilitates or instructs others in child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Any behaviour that places a child at risk of physical, emotional or sexual harm.

3. Reporting Child-Safety Concerns

Users, participating organisations and members of the public may report suspected CSAE, CSAM, grooming or other child-safety concerns using the contact details below.

Child Safety Contact
Email: support@benavilleglobal.com

Reports should include as much relevant information as possible. Do not download, forward or redistribute suspected illegal content in order to make a report.

4. Immediate Danger

Where a child is believed to be in immediate danger, contact the emergency services or the appropriate child-protection authority in your country without delay.

5. Investigation and Enforcement

NzuKonect may investigate reported or detected violations and take appropriate action, including:

  • Restricting access to relevant content or platform functions.
  • Removing prohibited content.
  • Suspending or permanently terminating accounts.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally required.
  • Notifying the participating organisation responsible for the account.
  • Reporting suspected criminal activity to law-enforcement or child-protection authorities where required or appropriate.

6. Cooperation With Authorities

NzuKonect will cooperate with valid legal requests and relevant authorities investigating suspected child sexual abuse, exploitation or endangerment.

7. Age Policy

The standard NzuKonect platform is intended for authorised users aged 18 and above.

Users under the age of 18 are not permitted to create independent accounts through the standard platform.

Participating organisations must not knowingly create accounts for children unless an approved safeguarding framework, an appropriate legal basis and any required parental or guardian consent are in place.

8. Responsibilities of Participating Organisations

Participating organisations are responsible for maintaining suitable safeguarding procedures and ensuring that their administrators use NzuKonect appropriately.

Organisations must also ensure that appropriate personnel are assigned to receive and respond to safeguarding concerns relevant to their members.

9. Data Preservation and Confidentiality

Information connected to a child-safety report may be preserved where necessary for investigation, safeguarding, legal compliance or cooperation with relevant authorities.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, subject to legal, safeguarding and enforcement obligations.

10. Review of These Standards

These standards may be updated to reflect changes in law, recognised safeguarding practice, platform functionality or operational requirements.

Policy ownership: These standards apply to the NzuKonect platform and are separate from any additional safeguarding policies maintained by individual participating organisations.
