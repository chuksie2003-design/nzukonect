Welcome

Welcome to the official support centre for NzuKonect.

NzuKonect is a Community Management Platform used by participating organisations to manage membership, payments, attendance, meetings, governance, records and communications.

Your organisation manages its own members, authorised administrators and organisational records within NzuKonect.

Frequently Asked Questions

I cannot sign in.

Confirm that you are using the email address registered with your participating organisation.

Use the Forgot Password option on the sign-in screen if you need to reset your password. Contact your organisation administrator if your registered email address is incorrect or your account has not yet been activated.

My personal information is incorrect.

Sign in and open the relevant profile or personal-details section. Update any fields that your organisation permits members to edit.

Contact your organisation administrator if the field is locked or cannot be changed.

My payment or dues history appears incorrect.

Financial records are maintained by authorised administrators of your participating organisation.

Contact the organisation’s finance officer or administrator if you believe a payment, donation or subscription has been omitted, duplicated or recorded incorrectly.

I cannot see my attendance records.

Attendance information may be published after an authorised administrator completes or confirms the relevant meeting record.

Contact your organisation administrator if an expected attendance record remains unavailable or appears inaccurate.

Why can I not access a feature that another member can see?

Access to NzuKonect features is controlled by organisation, membership status and assigned user role.

Some sections are limited to authorised administrators, finance officers, executives or other designated roles.

Is my information secure?

NzuKonect uses authentication, encrypted communication and role-based access controls designed to protect personal and organisational information.

Users should protect their passwords, avoid sharing account access and report suspected unauthorised activity promptly.

How do I delete my account or request deletion of my data?

Contact NzuKonect support and your participating organisation. Some organisational or financial records may need to be retained where required for legal, accounting, governance or dispute-resolution purposes.

Report a Problem

Include the following information when reporting a technical issue:

  • Your full name.
  • Your registered email address.
  • The name of your participating organisation.
  • Your device type, such as iPhone, iPad or Android.
  • Your operating-system version, where known.
  • Your NzuKonect app version, where known.
  • A clear description of the problem.
  • The steps that led to the problem.
  • The date and approximate time the issue occurred.
  • Screenshots or screen recordings where appropriate.

Do not include passwords, full payment-card details or other highly sensitive authentication information in a support message.
